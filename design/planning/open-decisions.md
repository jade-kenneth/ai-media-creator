### Open 19 review (2026-09-25)

Sources, read 2026-09-25: MiniMax Terms of Service and API Privacy Policy (both effective 2026-03-30, platform.minimax.io/protocol/terms-of-service, /privacy-policy), Paid Services Agreement (effective 2026-08-19, /protocol/paid-agreement), and the Video V2 API reference. The contracting entity is Nanonoble Pte. Ltd. (Singapore). Model availability is confirmed: `MiniMax-H3-Max` ran live on the account on 2026-09-25.

| Question | Finding | What it asks of the product |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Serving outputs to our users | Allowed. “As between you and us … you retain your ownership rights in Client input and generated content.” There is no commercial-use ban and no attribution duty. Limits: use only inside our own product, no standalone resale, and we are “solely responsible for all use of any AI Output by you and your end users”. | Nothing new. The required check (R20) already covers “reviewing … before using”. |
| Training and improvement | MiniMax “may use the input and generated content to provide, maintain, develop, and improve our Services”. No opt-out is published. No retention period is published (“as long as necessary”); task records can be queried for 7 days and a DELETE endpoint exists. | The privacy copy must say the photo and description may be used by the provider to improve its services. Ask MiniMax whether a no-training or zero-retention addendum exists. |
| Data location, law | Personal data is stored in the US. Singapore law, SIAC arbitration. | Privacy copy: an international transfer to the US. |
| AI-content labelling | The customer must “add non-intrusive identifiers, maintain log information … and place a prominent mark … within generated or edited content”. The H3 create endpoint has no watermark option, so the duty falls on us. | **Decision:** a visible “AI-generated” mark on scenes that use an AI clip in exports, plus a hidden identifier (e.g. MP4 metadata) and a log of which export used which clip. Not in §3.18 today. |
| Naming the provider | “Without prior written approval from us, you may not … disclose that we are your service provider.” The privacy policy built in Phase 22 names MiniMax. | **Decision:** get MiniMax's written approval, or describe it as “a third-party video generation service”. Legal should weigh this against privacy-law processor disclosure. |
| End users and takedown | We are responsible for end users' compliance, must hold end-user consent for Client Data, must act on a violation notice within 24 hours, and indemnify MiniMax. | Our Terms must flow down MiniMax's content rules (Terms page: decision 10). We need an ops path to disable a project's clips within 24 hours. |

Not confirmed: a DPA or no-training addendum, whether DELETE purges stored media, where API media is processed (and whether fal.ai is involved for H3-Max), and whether MiniMax embeds its own invisible identifier.

## Discrepancies with the earlier Claude Design batch

The earlier Batch 1 export (not in the repository) is superseded by the Design Reference. Deliberate changes:

- Batch 2 steps are hidden, not shown locked; the step strip reads “Step n of 5”.
- Session-expired copy no longer names the destination project, because a signed-out visitor can't be shown a private project title.
- Press feedback now scales buttons to 0.97, and dialogs exit over 120ms instead of instantly (design-engineering motion rules).
- The Terms link is not rendered until decision 10.
